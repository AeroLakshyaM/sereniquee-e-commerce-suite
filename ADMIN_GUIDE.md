# 🕯️ Sereniquee Candles - Admin Guide for Mom

## 🏠 Getting Started

When you log into the admin area, you'll see a **Home** tab that shows everything important at a glance:

### What You'll See on the Home Tab:
- **Today's Sales** 💰 - How much money you made today
- **Pending Orders** ⏰ - Orders that need your attention
- **Total Products** 📦 - How many candles you have in your store
- **Recent Orders** - The last few orders with their status

## 🔔 Order Notifications

### You'll Get Notified When:
- A new order comes in (you'll hear a sound! 🔊)
- The bell icon 🔔 in the top right will show a red badge with the number of new orders

### To Check Notifications:
1. Click the **bell icon** 🔔 in the top right corner
2. You'll see a list of recent orders
3. Click anywhere outside to close it

## 📦 Managing Orders (Orders Tab)

### Order Status Guide:
- **Pending** (Yellow) - New order, needs your attention
- **Processing** (Blue) - You're working on it
- **Shipped** (Purple) - Sent to customer
- **Delivered** (Green) - Customer received it
- **Cancelled** (Red) - Order was cancelled

### Easy Steps to Update an Order:
1. Go to **Orders** tab
2. Find the order you want to update
3. Click on it to expand details
4. Use the **Quick Status Update** buttons:
   - Click "Processing" when you start making the candles
   - Click "Shipped" when you send it
   - Click "Delivered" when customer confirms receipt

### To Print an Order:
1. Find the order
2. Click the **Print** button 🖨️ (printer icon)
3. A new window opens with the order details
4. Click "Print Order" button or use Ctrl+P

## ➕ Adding a New Product (Products Tab)

### Step-by-Step:
1. Go to **Products** tab
2. Click the purple **"Add Product"** button
3. Fill in the form:
   - **📸 Product Photo**: 
     - Click "Click to upload" to choose a photo from your computer
     - OR paste a link to an online image
   - **🏷️ Product Name**: e.g., "Lavender Dreams Candle"
   - **📝 Description**: Tell customers what makes it special
   - **💰 Price**: Enter the price (e.g., 29.99)
   - **📦 Stock**: How many you have available
   - **🏪 Category**: e.g., "signature", "seasonal", "gift"
   - **⭐ Featured**: Turn ON if you want it on the homepage
4. Click **"✨ Add Product"** button

### Editing an Existing Product:
1. Go to **Products** tab
2. Find the product in the table
3. Click the **pencil icon** ✏️
4. Make your changes
5. Click **"💾 Save Changes"**

### Deleting a Product:
1. Go to **Products** tab
2. Find the product
3. Click the **trash icon** 🗑️
4. Confirm you want to delete it

## 📊 Analytics Tab

This shows you:
- Total revenue (all-time sales)
- Number of orders
- Number of customers
- Charts and graphs of your sales

## 💡 Quick Tips

### Best Practices:
✅ Check the **Home** tab every morning to see pending orders
✅ Update order status as soon as you ship items
✅ Keep product stock numbers updated
✅ Add clear, beautiful photos of your candles
✅ Write descriptions that tell a story about each scent

### If Something Goes Wrong:
- If an image won't upload, just paste the image URL instead
- If an order update doesn't save, try again
- All changes are saved automatically to the database

### Getting Help:
- Each form has helpful hints in gray text
- Hover over buttons to see what they do
- Can't find something? Look through all the tabs!

## 🎯 Daily Workflow Suggestion

**Morning Routine:**
1. Log into admin
2. Check **Home** tab for new orders
3. Click on any **Pending** orders
4. Start preparing them (click "Processing")

**After Making Candles:**
1. Go to **Products** tab
2. Update stock quantities for products you made
3. Add any new candle types

**After Shipping:**
1. Go to **Orders** tab
2. Find shipped orders
3. Click "Shipped" button
4. Print order for your records

**End of Day:**
1. Check **Analytics** tab
2. See how much you made today!
3. Plan tomorrow's production

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: How do I know if an order is paid?**
A: All orders in the system are already paid! The payment happens before you see them.

**Q: Can customers cancel orders?**
A: They can contact you to request cancellation. Then you can change the status to "Cancelled".

**Q: What if I run out of a product?**
A: Set the stock to 0 in the Products tab. It will show "Out of Stock" to customers.

**Q: How do I offer discounts?**
A: Edit the product price temporarily, or contact your developer to add a coupon system.
