# Blog Content Formatting Guide

## What Changed?

The AI now generates blog posts with **proper formatting** that looks beautiful when people read them!

---

## How It Works Now

### ✅ Before (Old - Messy with hashtags):
```
## This Was A Heading
**bold text** that didn't show properly
### Another heading
```

### ✅ After (New - Clean & Readable):
```
SECTION: This Is A Beautiful Heading

Your paragraph text here. BOLD: Important information stands out naturally.

SECTION: Another Heading

More content with proper formatting.
```

---

### When AI generates content with:
```
SECTION: 5 Ways to Care for Your Candles
```

## Example Generated Blog

**What AI Creates:**
```
There's nothing quite like the warm glow of a handcrafted candle filling your home with beautiful fragrance. At Sereniquee, we pour each candle with love and care.

SECTION: Why Candle Care Matters

Taking care of your luxury candles ensures they burn beautifully for hours. BOLD: Proper maintenance can double the life of your candle while keeping the fragrance strong and consistent.

The key is understanding a few simple principles. Every candle, whether made from soy wax or beeswax, benefits from proper care.

SECTION: The Number One Rule

BOLD: Always trim your wick to 1/4 inch before lighting. This prevents black smoke, uneven burning, and ensures a clean, beautiful flame every time.

Many people skip this step, but it truly makes all the difference. We recommend using a proper wick trimmer for the best results.
```

**What Your Readers See:**

---

There's nothing quite like the warm glow of a handcrafted candle filling your home with beautiful fragrance. At Sereniquee, we pour each candle with love and care.

## Why Candle Care Matters

Taking care of your luxury candles ensures they burn beautifully for hours. **Proper maintenance can double the life of your candle** while keeping the fragrance strong and consistent.

The key is understanding a few simple principles. Every candle, whether made from soy wax or beeswax, benefits from proper care.

## The Number One Rule

**Always trim your wick to 1/4 inch before lighting.** This prevents black smoke, uneven burning, and ensures a clean, beautiful flame every time.

Many people skip this step, but it truly makes all the difference. We recommend using a proper wick trimmer for the best results.

## If You Want to Add Formatting Manually

If you write content yourself (not using AI), you can add:

**For a heading:**
```
SECTION: Your Heading Text
```

**For bold text:**
```
This is normal text with BOLD: important emphasis here.
```

But honestly, the AI does this automatically, so you rarely need to! 😊
