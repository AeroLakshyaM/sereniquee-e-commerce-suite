## 🎯 Feature Summary

### What It Does:
Allows you to add detailed, professional product descriptions with:
- Technical specifications (weight, dimensions, burn time)
- Scent profiles (top/heart/base notes)
- Care instructions
- Material information
- Formatted text (bold headings, bullet points)

### Where It Appears:
- **Admin Dashboard:** Edit/add full descriptions with preview
- **Product Pages:** Beautiful accordion with formatted content
- **SEO:** Indexed by search engines for better rankings

### Benefits:
✅ Professional product pages  
✅ Better SEO rankings  
✅ Increased customer trust  
✅ Higher conversion rates  
✅ Reduced support questions  
✅ Fewer product returns
