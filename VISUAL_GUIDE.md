## Real Example

### Input:
```
Topic: How to care for scented candles

Additional Notes:
- Trim the wick to 1/4 inch
- Don't burn for more than 4 hours
- Keep away from drafts
- My candles are made with soy wax
```

```
5 Essential Tips to Care for Your Scented Candles
```

**Tags:**

## Color Coding Guide

- 🟦 **Blue Box** = AI Generator (at top)
- 🟩 **Green Text** = Auto-filled by AI (editable)
- 🟨 **Yellow Text** = You need to fill (images, author)
- 🟪 **Purple Button** = Generate with AI
- 🟥 **Blue Button** = Publish Blog

## Icon Legend

- ✨ = AI/Magic feature
- 🪄 = Generate action
- ⏳ = Loading/Processing
- 💡 = Tip/Helpful info
- ⚠️ = Important instruction
- 📊 = Statistics (word count, reading time)
- 📤 = Publish/Submit
- ☑ = Toggle/Switch
- 🕯️ = Candle/Brand related
