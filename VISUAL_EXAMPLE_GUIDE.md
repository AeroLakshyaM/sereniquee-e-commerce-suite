## 🎯 Real-World Example

### Product: "Lavender Dreams Candle"

#### What You Type in Admin:
```markdown
**Elevate your sanctuary with the subtle art of fragrance.**

Meticulously hand-poured in small batches, this candle represents the intersection of sustainable luxury and artisanal craftsmanship. We use only 100% natural soy wax blended with premium fragrance oils to ensure a non-toxic, soot-free burn that is safe for your home and family.

**Technical Specifications**

* Net Weight: 200g / 7 oz
* Dimensions: 8.5 cm (Height) x 7 cm (Diameter)
* Burn Time: Approximately 40-45 hours
* Wax Type: 100% Natural Eco-Friendly Soy Wax
* Wick Type: Lead-free, braided cotton for a clean burn
* Vessel Material: High-grade heat-resistant frosted glass
* Fragrance Load: High-concentration for optimal scent throw

**Scent Profile**

* Top Notes: Fresh Lavender, Bergamot
* Heart Notes: French Lavender Fields, White Tea
* Base Notes: Vanilla Bean, Soft Musk

A serene blend designed to promote relaxation and peaceful sleep. The calming properties of lavender combine with subtle vanilla undertones to create a spa-like atmosphere in any room.

**Candle Care**

* Trim the wick to 1/4 inch before every light to prevent smoking.
* Allow the wax pool to reach the edges of the jar during the first burn to prevent tunneling.
* Burn for 2-4 hours at a time for optimal performance.
* Never leave a burning candle unattended.
* Keep away from children and pets.
```

## 📊 Formatting Examples

### Bold Text
**Input:** `**Technical Specifications**`  
**Output:** **Technical Specifications** (bold heading)

### Bullet Points
**Input:** `* Net Weight: 200g`  
**Output:** • Net Weight: 200g

**Input:** `- Top Notes: Lavender`  
**Output:** • Top Notes: Lavender

### Paragraphs
**Input:**
```
First paragraph text.

Second paragraph text.
```
**Output:**
```
First paragraph text.
↕️ (space)
Second paragraph text.
```

## 💡 Comparison: Product Card vs Product Page

### Product Card (Grid/List View)
- Shows: **Short Description** (brief 1-2 sentences)
- Character limit: ~150 chars
- Example: "Hand-poured soy candle with calming lavender. 40+ hour burn time."

### Product Detail Page
- Shows: **Full Description** (complete details)
- Character limit: None (500-1500 words recommended)
- Includes: Specs, scent profile, care instructions, materials

**Both are important!** Use short description for browsing, full description for detailed information.

## 🎯 What Makes a Good Full Description?

### ✅ Good Example:
- Specific measurements (200g, 8.5cm, 40-45 hours)
- Clear scent breakdown (top/heart/base notes)
- Safety instructions included
- Professional tone
- Easy to scan with sections and bullets

### ❌ Avoid:
- Vague measurements ("medium size")
- No scent details ("smells nice")
- Missing safety info
- Too casual tone
- Long walls of text without formatting
